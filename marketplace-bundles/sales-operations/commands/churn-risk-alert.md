# Churn Risk Alert

Identifies customer accounts at risk of churn and generates intervention plans.

## Features

- Automated churn risk scoring
- Multi-factor risk assessment
- Segment-specific alert thresholds
- Intervention action plans
- Customer success team routing
- Escalation workflows
- Historical churn pattern matching
- Risk trend tracking

## Usage

Run churn risk analysis:

```
/churn-risk-alert
```

Or with specific criteria:

```
/churn-risk-alert Focus on accounts with monthly support tickets >5
/churn-risk-alert Alert only on critical risk (score > 80)
/churn-risk-alert Segment by industry and show top risks per segment
```

## Output

Generates:
- List of at-risk accounts with risk scores
- Risk factors breakdown per account
- Recommended intervention actions
- Customer success team assignments
- Timeline for follow-up
- Expected revenue at risk
- Historical context (previous risks)

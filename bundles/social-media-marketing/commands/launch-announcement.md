# Launch Announcement

Creates a coordinated, multi-channel launch announcement for a software
product or feature, with platform-native copy and a timed rollout.

## Features

- Core launch narrative and key messages
- Platform-native variants (X thread, LinkedIn post, short-form script)
- Pre-launch teaser and post-launch follow-up copy
- Product Hunt / Hacker News / community kit
- Founder and team amplification copy
- Clear CTAs and tracking-ready links
- Visual and demo asset suggestions
- Timed rollout sequence

## Usage

Generate a launch announcement:

```
/launch-announcement
```

Or with specific focus:

```
/launch-announcement Feature: SSO support, target enterprise buyers on LinkedIn
/launch-announcement Major release, lead with a hero demo video on X
/launch-announcement Include a Product Hunt tagline and first comment
```

## Output

Generates:
- Headline announcement and core message
- Channel-specific copy (X thread, LinkedIn, short-form script)
- Pre-launch teasers and post-launch recap posts
- Community/launch-platform kit (tagline, description, first comment)
- Founder and team amplification snippets
- Rollout timeline (T-minus teasers → launch → follow-up)
- CTA and link checklist

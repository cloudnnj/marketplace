# Content Calendar

Generates a structured social media content calendar for a software startup,
balancing content pillars across channels and dates.

## Features

- Multi-week, multi-channel editorial calendar
- Content pillar balancing (education, product, founder POV, social proof, community)
- Per-post hooks, angles, and CTAs
- Repurposing chains (one idea → thread → clip → carousel)
- Launch and event sequencing
- Cadence aligned to team capacity
- Audience targeting per post (developer vs. buyer)
- Draft-ready post stubs

## Usage

Generate a content calendar:

```
/content-calendar
```

Or with specific focus:

```
/content-calendar 6 weeks, prioritize LinkedIn thought leadership
/content-calendar Plan around our Product Hunt launch on the 15th
/content-calendar Daily X posts plus 2 LinkedIn posts per week
```

## Output

Generates:
- Week-by-week calendar grid (date, channel, pillar, format)
- Hook and CTA for each scheduled post
- Repurposing map linking related posts
- Launch/event momentum sequence
- Cadence summary and balance check by pillar and channel
- Draft stubs ready to hand to copywriting

# Social Copywriting

Platform-native copywriting for software startup social channels.

## Capabilities

- Write scroll-stopping hooks and opening lines
- Draft X/Twitter posts and multi-tweet threads
- Compose LinkedIn posts for founders and economic buyers
- Script short-form video (Reels/Shorts/TikTok) and carousels
- Adapt one core message into platform-specific variants
- Tune voice for developer vs. business-buyer audiences
- Add clear, low-friction CTAs (try, star, sign up, reply)
- Generate A/B hook and headline variations

## Usage

This skill turns product features, changelogs, and ideas into ready-to-post,
channel-appropriate social copy.

## Features

- Hook libraries and proven post formats
- Per-platform length and tone constraints
- Thread and carousel structuring
- CTA frameworks for each funnel stage
- Repurposing chains (blog → thread → clip → carousel)
- A/B variant generation
- Hashtag and mention guidance
- Brand voice consistency checks

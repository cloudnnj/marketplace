# Campaign Analytics

Performance measurement and reporting for social media campaigns.

## Capabilities

- Track reach, impressions, and engagement rate by post and channel
- Monitor follower growth and audience quality over time
- Attribute website visits, signups, and trials to social sources
- Calculate cost per follower, per lead, and per signup
- Benchmark content pillars and formats against each other
- Identify top-performing posts and replicable patterns
- Measure share of voice vs. competitors
- Generate weekly and campaign-level performance reports

## Usage

This skill analyzes social media performance data and turns it into
actionable recommendations for the next content cycle.

## Features

- Multi-channel metrics aggregation
- UTM and conversion attribution modeling
- Content-pillar and format performance breakdown
- Funnel reporting (impression → click → signup)
- ROI and CAC contribution analysis
- Trend detection and anomaly flagging
- Competitor share-of-voice tracking
- Executive and team-level dashboards
